My research is in behavioral and experimental economics, cultural economics and political economy. In terms of broad topic areas, I have worked on preference uncertainty and complexity, consumer decision making, heterogeneity in moral values, and cultural variation in economic and social preferences. Methodologically, my work relies on a combination of experiments, multinational surveys, formal theory, and machine learning techniques to extract complexity features.
